# Privacy Policy for Ebb

**Last updated: 10/09/2026**

## The short version

Ebb does not collect anything. It has no servers, no analytics, no
accounts, and no third-party SDKs. Your goals stay on your device and,
if you turn on sync, in your own iCloud account.

## What Ebb stores, and where

Everything you create in Ebb — goal names, emoji, start and end dates,
visualization choices, and reminder settings — is stored locally on
your device.

If you have Ebb Pro and iCloud sync enabled, that same data is also
stored in **your** private iCloud database. That database belongs to
your Apple Account. Apple provides the storage; we cannot read it, and
neither can anyone else without your Apple Account credentials.

Your app preferences (such as your default visualization) are stored on
your device.

## What Ebb does not do

- No analytics or crash reporting SDKs
- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
- No user accounts, sign-in, or email collection
- No tracking across apps or websites
- No selling or sharing of data, because there is none to sell

Ebb makes no network requests of its own. The only network activity is
Apple's: iCloud sync, if you enable it, and the App Store when you
purchase or restore Ebb Pro.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device using
iOS's local notification system. Nothing about your goals is
transmitted anywhere to produce them. You can revoke notification
permission at any time in iOS Settings.

## Purchases

Ebb Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple's App Store. Ebb
receives only whether a valid purchase exists for your Apple Account.
Payment details are never seen by, or transmitted to, Ebb.

## Children

Ebb is not directed at children and collects no personal information
from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a goal removes it from your device and, if sync is on, from
your iCloud database. Deleting the app removes all local data. To
remove synced data, use **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage
Account Storage** on your device.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this
address with a new date above. Because Ebb collects nothing, any change
is likely to be a clarification rather than a change in practice.
